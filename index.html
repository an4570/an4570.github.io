About

I'm a student at NYU Stern studying Business, Technology, and Entrepreneurship and I've already been running a media company for over six years. At 20, I manage Slat Entertainment Ltd., a multi-channel YouTube network specializing in gaming and VR content that's racked up over 500 million views.

"Been watching YouTube since I was literally 4 years old... I'm so, so happy and grateful to be doing this today."

Day to day, that means overseeing production pipelines, coaching a global team of 30+ editors and creators, obsessing over retention curves and click-through rates, and negotiating brand deals. I think of content strategy the way engineers think about systems: build the right inputs, and the outputs scale.

Beyond Slat, I'm leading marketing for Cycl, a cycle-based supplement tracking app for women that personalizes nutrition around hormonal changes. Now available on the App Store, Cycl tackles a problem I noticed wasn't being solved thoughtfully. I'm always looking for the next thing worth building.
